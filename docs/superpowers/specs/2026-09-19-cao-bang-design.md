# Cao Bằng valley — design

A travel showcase (no sender/recipient): one continuous 3D valley to explore freely.

- **Where**: `t/cao-bang/`, self-contained (copies rig/tween/figures from Trung Thu, no shared lib).
- **Valley** (north → south along the Quây Sơn river): Bản Giốc falls (two tiers on a horseshoe cliff, rafts in the
  pool); rice fields and haystack karst peaks; a Tày/Nùng village on terraces (stilt houses, bamboo water wheel,
  Phia Thắp incense fans, chestnut trees); Mắt Thần mountain across the fields; the then-singing yard in the village.
- **Explore**: orbit overview (drag turn, pinch/wheel zoom), tap ground to glide there, tap a landmark label to fly in
  and open its bilingual fact card, "Toàn cảnh" returns to the overview.
- **Time**: golden afternoon; a sun/moon button (and visiting the then yard) fades to dusk: fire, lamps, crickets,
  đàn tính.
- **Sound**: synthesized; falls roar by distance, birds by day, crickets + đàn tính at dusk.
- **Facts**: Bản Giốc, Quây Sơn, Mắt Thần, karst geopark, stilt house, water wheel, Phia Thắp incense, chestnuts, then.
- **Listing**: `templates.json`, new "Travel" category.
